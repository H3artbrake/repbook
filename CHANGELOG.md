# v1.0.1 — Glute Focus and workout targets

- Adds Glute Focus: hip thrust 3 × 8, Bulgarian split squat 3 × 8 per leg, cable glute kickback 2 × 12 per leg, standing cable hip abduction 2 × 15 per leg. No abduction machine required.
- Starter Day 1–3 templates now include recommended sets and reps: most compound lifts 3 × 8; curls 2 × 8; triceps isolation 2 × 10; lateral raises and rear-delt fly 2 × 12.
- Edit recommended sets and reps in any workout template. Leave both blank to omit a target.
- Starting a template creates the recommended number of blank set rows. Suggested reps appear as placeholders; only reps you enter count toward history and progress.
- Existing accounts receive the new template and missing starter recommendations once on startup. Edited names, exercise order, history, active sessions and accounts are preserved. Deleted starter days are not recreated.
- Single-side reps are per side. Glute Focus allows roughly an hour including warm-up, rest and equipment changes. Rest 2–3 minutes for hip thrusts, 90–120 seconds after both legs of split squats and 60–90 seconds after both sides of cable exercises. Warm-up sets are additional.
